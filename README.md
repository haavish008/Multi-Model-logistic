# AI-Based Multi-Level Logistics Optimization

An intelligent logistics optimization platform integrating Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Geospatial Analysis, and Advanced Pathfinding Algorithms to improve transportation efficiency, minimize operational risks, and enable adaptive route planning.

## Overview

AI-Based Multi-Level Logistics Optimization is designed to address complex transportation and logistics challenges through intelligent route planning and geospatial decision-making.

The system analyzes road networks, geographical conditions, environmental factors, and potential disruptions to identify efficient routes and alternative paths under changing conditions.

The platform aims to optimize multiple logistics parameters, including travel time, distance, fuel consumption, transportation cost, and route risk.

## Key Features

- **Intelligent Route Optimization:** Identifies efficient transportation routes using advanced pathfinding algorithms.
- **Geospatial Intelligence:** Integrates geographical datasets and spatial analysis for location-aware decision-making.
- **Risk Assessment:** Evaluates environmental and geographical risks, including rainfall and landslide exposure.
- **Adaptive Rerouting:** Supports alternative route planning when existing routes become unavailable or unsafe.
- **Weather-Aware Planning:** Incorporates rainfall and environmental conditions into route analysis.
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Making:** Evaluates competing route alternatives using optimization and decision-making techniques.
- **Interactive Visualization:** Provides geographical visualization of transportation networks and optimized routes.
